# Changelog

Newest first. The top section is published as the release notes and shown in the app (SYSTEM > Updates).

## 0.15 - Queue and polish
- Seven rich theme styles, replacing the first set: Dreamscape (violet dusk with peach glow and glass tiles), Cobalt Coral (electric blue fading to coral), Black Amber (black glass with molten amber), Porcelain (soft raised light style), Glass Navy (frosted glass with gold), Rose Noir (black wine with rose gold) and Pearl Aurora (light pastel glass). Each has a gradient background with glows and scenery, its own card finish (glass, drop shadow or raised), button shape and colours. Pick one in CONFIG > Appearance > Style; Classic keeps the dark, light, system and accent choices.
- Call results are clearer: besides answered / no answer / failed, a call that ends fast without an answer is now labelled busy or declined, and the history shows how long unanswered calls rang.
- Smart gaps: a different wait after a busy line and after no answer (CONFIG > Timing).
- Redial until answered, with a most-tries cap and a give-up time (CONFIG > Answers).
- Answer alert: a sound and vibration when a call is still going after the time you set, with an option to keep the call, open the mic and switch to speaker or earpiece (CONFIG > Answer alert). Android does not report the moment of answer, so this is an estimate; LEARN FROM HISTORY sets it from your own unanswered calls.
- LOG > STATS: answer rate overall, by time of day and by number, plus your best hour.
- Four premium floating looks, Aurora, Gold, Carbon and Frost, with gradient rings, a glass highlight and a styled floating panel. Pick one in CONFIG > Floating bubble; PREVIEW LOOK shows the panel and the bubble.
- Names show in the run notification and the floating bubble, and the queue has a search box (name or number, local or international form) once it has 6 or more numbers.
- SELECT mode in the queue: tick several numbers, then DELETE them or give them the same settings. CLEAR and bulk delete can be undone.
- Friendlier empty states for the queue and the LOG tab.
- Less of a flash at launch in light mode (the window now starts in your theme colour).
- Fixed: the microphone no longer stays muted when you take over a call. KEEP CALL now opens the mic, and a new UNMUTE button (app and notification) does it for calls without a time limit.
- Tap a number to give it a name (the target dialog now has a Name field); unnamed rows say "tap to add a name".
- During a run, each waiting number has a pause button: it is skipped while on hold and called as soon as you resume it. If only held numbers are left, the run waits for you.
- CONFIG is regrouped into CALLING, SAFETY and APP, with folding cards that remember whether they were open.
- SIM strategy now sits next to the SIM chooser on DIAL.
- A red dot on SYSTEM when something needs permission, and short descriptions on cards.
- Drag the handle on a queue row (press and hold) to reorder numbers.
- Swipe a queue row sideways to remove it, with a 5 second UNDO.
- New hand-drawn tab icons.
- Haptic feedback can be switched off (CONFIG > Appearance).
- Notification buttons now match the moment: Resume while paused, Keep call or Auto hang-up during a timed call.
- Updates: the app shows what is new before you install.
- Version name now follows the build number.
- Light theme, plus a "follow system" option (CONFIG > Appearance > Theme).
- New notification icon (handset with redial arrow).
- New app icon: white handset with a redial arrow on blue (with a themed-icon version).
