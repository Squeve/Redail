# Changelog

Newest first. The top section is published as the release notes and shown in the app (SYSTEM > Updates).

## 0.15 - Queue and polish
- CONFIG is regrouped into CALLING, SAFETY and APP, with folding cards that remember whether they were open.
- SIM strategy now sits next to the SIM chooser on DIAL.
- A red dot on SYSTEM when something needs permission, and short descriptions on cards.
- Drag the handle on a queue row (press and hold) to reorder numbers.
- Swipe a queue row sideways to remove it, with a 5 second UNDO.
- New hand-drawn tab icons.
- Haptic feedback can be switched off (CONFIG > Appearance).
- Notification buttons now match the moment: Resume while paused, Keep call or Auto hang-up during a timed call.
- Updates: the app shows what is new before you install.
- Version name now follows the build number.
- Light theme, plus a "follow system" option (CONFIG > Appearance > Theme).
- New notification icon (handset with redial arrow).
- New app icon: white handset with a redial arrow on blue (with a themed-icon version).
